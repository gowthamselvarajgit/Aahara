# Data Sources

## Primary Indian Source: ICMR-NIN IFCT 2017
- **Name**: Indian Food Composition Tables 2017
- **URL**: ICMR-NIN portal
- **License**: Government data. REQUIRES LEGAL REVIEW. (Requires attribution, freely usable for public health/reference, but commercial redistribution requires explicit legal clearance).
- **Strengths**: Highly accurate for Tamil/Indian regional foods (Idli, Dosa, Sambars, raw vs cooked).

## Secondary Source: USDA FoodData Central
- **Name**: Foundation Foods & SR Legacy
- **URL**: fdc.nal.usda.gov
- **License**: Public Domain (CC0)
- **Strengths**: Extensive, completely free to use, distribute, and modify. Excellent for raw ingredients, meats, and generic items.

## Rejected Sources
- **HealthifyMe / Hevy Databases**: Proprietary, scraped, or copyrighted. Explicitly rejected to maintain legal defensibility.
